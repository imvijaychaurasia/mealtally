# Mealtally brand assets

Built from the chosen ChatGPT concept (v1), redrawn as clean vectors on a 512-unit grid.

## Files
- `logo-horizontal.svg`: icon and wordmark, for headers and the README. `logo-horizontal-mono.svg` is the one-colour version.
- `icon.svg`: the main icon. Variants:
  - `icon-mono.svg` for one-colour printing such as receipts and stamps
  - `icon-reverse.svg` for use on a green background
- `app-icon.svg`: the rounded-square icon for the PWA and app stores.
- `favicon.svg`: a simplified icon with three tines, for 16–48 px.
- `wordmark.svg`: "mealtally" in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, **converted to outlines**, so it needs no font.
- PNG exports:
  - icon: 1024, 512 and 192
  - app icon: 1024, 512 and 180 (Apple touch icon)
  - favicon: 48, 32 and 16
  - horizontal logo: 1600 wide
- `font/OFL.txt`: the licence of the font used in the wordmark (SIL Open Font License).

## Colours
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Curry-leaf green (ring, fork) | `#1F7A4D` |
| Saffron (tally stroke) | `#F2A007` |
| Cream (plate) | `#FFF8EE` |
| Tamarind (wordmark, one-colour) | `#2A1C14` |

## Usage
- **Clear space:** keep at least the ring's thickness (36/512 of the icon height) clear on every side.
- **Minimum sizes:** the main icon from 32 px, the favicon for anything smaller, and the horizontal logo from 120 px wide.
- **Don't** recolour the tally stroke, add effects, rotate it, stretch it, or put the full-colour icon on a busy photo.
